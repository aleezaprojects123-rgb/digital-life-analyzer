# 4. The Five Market Differentiators

| **#** | **Feature** | **What it does** | **Why it wins** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Predictive Distraction Forecast | Per-user ML model scores every hour of today for distraction risk; shown as a heatmap on the dashboard | Only tool that answers 'when will I lose focus?' instead of 'where did it go?' |
| 2 | Preemptive Nudge | Desktop notification ~5 min before a predicted risk window, suggesting a focus block; snoozable, self-cooling | Acts before focus breaks — the behavior-change moment |
| 3 | On-Device AI Categorization | Quantized model (ONNX Runtime) inside the agent classifies by meaning; works unplugged | 'AI with zero data leaving your PC' — kills the trust objection and demos like magic |
| 4 | Time Recovery Calculator | Lost switching/entertainment time → recoverable hours in relatable units + 3-action weekly plan | Turns guilt into a concrete, measurable plan |
| 5 | Weekly Wrapped + Calendar Sync | Shareable story-style weekly recap; best focus hours pushed to Google/Outlook as focus blocks in one click | Viral loop (Wrapped) + insight-to-action (calendar) |
