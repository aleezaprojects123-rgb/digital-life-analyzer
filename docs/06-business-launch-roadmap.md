# Digital Life Analyzer — Project Document

*Project Document — v2.0 Market Launch Edition | Windows (v1)*

# 7. Business Model

Free: tracking, timeline, reports, score, goal, Weekly Wrapped.

Pro ($4–6/month): distraction forecast + nudges, recovery plans, calendar sync, unlimited history.

No ads, no data resale. Privacy is the brand.

# 8. Launch Plan

| **Phase** | **Duration** | **Exit criteria** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot | Weeks 1–4 | 10–15 daily users; forecast precision ≥60%; real demo footage captured |
| Private beta | Weeks 5–8 | 100 users; week-4 retention ≥35%; first 20 paying users |
| Public launch | Week 9+ | Product Hunt, Reddit, Wrapped-share loop; feedback → v1.1 |

# 9. Post-Launch Roadmap

v1.1: macOS agent, drift alerts, deep-focus wellbeing signals.

v2: Android collector, Ask Your Data (local LLM), habit experiments, team/study-group mode.

*Cut from launch scope and why: self-experiments (research toy), task auto-detection (no paid demand signal), Ask Your Data chat (every app has one in 2026 — not a differentiator).*
