# 1. Summary

Digital Life Analyzer is not another time tracker. Trackers tell you where your time went — after it is gone. We tell you where it is about to go, protect your focus before it breaks, and prove the recovered hours every week.

A browser extension and a Windows agent record activity, merge it into one timeline, and categorize it using an AI model that runs entirely on the user's computer. A web dashboard shows reports, a distraction forecast for today, and a weekly Wrapped.

All five market differentiators are covered in Section 6: Predictive Distraction Forecast, On-Device AI Categorization, Time Recovery Calculator, Weekly Wrapped, and Best-Hours Calendar Sync.

# 2. Problem

People do not know where their time goes — and existing tools only confirm the loss after the day is over.

RescueTime, Toggl, and ManicTime report time per app or site. None predict distraction, and none act before focus is lost.

Cloud-based tools upload detailed activity, which users increasingly distrust.
